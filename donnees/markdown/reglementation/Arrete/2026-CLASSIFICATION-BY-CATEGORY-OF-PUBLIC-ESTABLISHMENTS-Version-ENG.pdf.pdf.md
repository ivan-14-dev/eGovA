HEREBYORDERS AS FOLLOWS:

ARTICLE 1.- (1) This Order classifies by category Public Establishments in Cameroon for the
performance achieved for the 2022, 2023 and 2024 fiscal years.

(2) The Public Establishments referred to in paragraph 1 above concern:

traditional public establishments, regardless of the form chosen, whose management is
subject to the fiscal regime of the State;

public establishments of a special nature, operating in the form of a public enterprise, in
particular with regard to accounting and financial management.
